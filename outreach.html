Girls' Math and Coding Day at JMU

Dr. Oh is the creator and organizer of this annual outreach event for students in the Harrisonburg City Public Schools Advancement Via Individual Determination (AVID) program. Since 2020, approximately 30 students have been attending this outreach event each year, where they take part in a Python programming workshop and go to talks by female students and faculty at JMU.
